### Where to Add Copyright in Play Console:

1. **Store Listing** → **Main Store Listing** → **Privacy Policy** section
   - Add copyright notice at the bottom of your app description (optional)

2. **Store Listing** → **Store Settings** → **Developer Name**
   - Set as: "ODA Transportation" or "ODA Black Car Service"

3. **App Content** → **Privacy Policy**
   - Copyright can be included in Privacy Policy document

### Standard Copyright Format:

For Google Play Store, you typically don't need a separate "copyright" field, but you can:

1. **Include in App Description** (optional, at the bottom):
   ```
   ...
   
   © 2025 ODA Transportation. All rights reserved.
   ```

2. **In Developer Information**:
   - **Developer Name**: ODA Transportation
   - **Email**: info@odatransportation.com (from your codebase)
   - **Website**: https://odatransportation.com

3. **In Privacy Policy** (recommended):
   - Include copyright notice in your Privacy Policy document
