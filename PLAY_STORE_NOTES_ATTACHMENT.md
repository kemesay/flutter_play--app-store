# Google Play Console - Notes & Attachment Guide

## Notes Field (4000 character limit)

### Recommended Notes Content:

```
ODA Black Car Service - Mobile Application

APPLICATION OVERVIEW:
This is a transportation booking application for ODA Black Car Service, providing premium black car transportation services in Los Angeles and Anaheim, California. The app allows users to book three types of services: airport transfers, point-to-point rides, and hourly charter services.

KEY FUNCTIONALITY:
- User registration and authentication
- Booking management (create, view, and track bookings)
- Real-time route planning using Google Maps
- Payment processing with credit card support
- Trip history and account management
- Location services for pickup/drop-off address selection

PERMISSIONS EXPLANATION:
- Location Services: Required to select pickup and drop-off addresses, calculate routes, and provide real-time navigation. Users can manually enter addresses if location services are disabled.
- Internet: Required for booking services, payment processing, and syncing data with our backend server.
- Network State: Used to check connectivity status and provide offline functionality where possible.

TARGET AUDIENCE:
Business travelers, airport passengers, event organizers, corporate clients, and individuals requiring premium transportation services in the Los Angeles and Orange County areas.

BUSINESS MODEL:
Users pay for transportation services through the app using secure payment processing. Services are provided by ODA Black Car Service licensed transportation company.

CONTENT RATINGS:
The app contains no inappropriate content. All content is suitable for all ages as it is a transportation booking service.

PRIVACY POLICY:
A privacy policy is available at our website (https://odatransportation.com/) explaining how user data is collected and used. User data collected includes: name, email, phone number, booking history, and payment information (processed securely through third-party payment processors).

TESTING INFORMATION:
- App has been tested on Android devices running Android 8.0 (API 26) and higher
- Tested on various screen sizes including phones and tablets
- All core booking and payment features have been verified

ADDITIONAL NOTES:
- The app integrates with Google Maps for route planning and address selection
- Payment processing is handled through secure third-party services
- All driver information and vehicle details are managed through our backend system
- Users can track their bookings in real-time through the app
```

## Recommended: Leave Attachment Empty (if optional)

For most Google Play Store submissions, **you can leave the attachment field empty** unless:
- Google specifically requests additional documentation
- You have special requirements that need explanation
- You're submitting a particularly complex app with unique features

**Best Practice:** Only attach files if explicitly required or if you have critical information that cannot be communicated in the Notes field.

---

## Important Reminders:

1. **Notes are for Google reviewers** - Explain any special permissions, features, or requirements
2. **Keep it professional** - Use clear, concise language
3. **Be specific** - Explain permissions, business model, and functionality clearly
4. **Don't repeat store listing** - Focus on technical/administrative details reviewers need
5. **Character limit** - Notes field has 4000 character limit (you have plenty of room)
